WHAT THE APP IS
A Sims-style build-mode room designer: a customer draws a room (or taps
"Quick 5 × 4 m room"), then places wellness/fitness products from a merchant catalog
onto the floor plan (0.5 m snap grid), sees a live cost total, and sends the design
to the PPW team via "Request quote". Revenue model: referral commission to merchants
(K1-Sport is the launch merchant) — the app never takes payment itself in this flow.
