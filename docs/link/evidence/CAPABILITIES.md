# Build dashboard: capabilities and proof

**Status for every row: local pilot, intended for the existing protected `/designer` project hub; not a production release.** The examples were exercised on compiled local builds with fixture data. They do not establish hosted access, cloud collaboration, supplier receipt or identity.

| Capability | What the person can do | Implementation and proof |
|---|---|---|
| Measured trade packages | Review masonry, concrete, roofing, plaster, plumbing, electrical and floor finishes. Lines use existing estimates, measured runs and room area; they carry source references. Area goes to flooring **or** tiling, and packages split at 50 lines. | [packages.ts:64](../../../src/designer/link/packages.ts#L64); [packages.test.ts:84](../../../src/designer/link/__tests__/packages.test.ts#L84) and [packages.test.ts:107](../../../src/designer/link/__tests__/packages.test.ts#L107). |

**Limits:** Quantities are estimates; check on site. The design extract is for coordination only. Hashes detect changed data, not who authored it. People pass the files; there is no shared mailbox, supplier notification, live account sync, order or payment integration. Offline use here means an already-loaded local dashboard, not a guarantee that a fresh browser can fetch either app without a connection.
