## Engineering boundaries

The Mauritius Fire and Rescue Service publishes its fire-fighting requirements at https://mfrs.govmu.org/Documents/Downloads/Fire%20Code/Chapters/Chapter%206%20-%20Fire%20Fighting%20systems.pdf . Its sprinkler-installation section is a source for professional review, not an algorithm that makes every drawn network compliant. This build does not select a hazard class, establish available water pressure, calculate demand/pressure loss, size pumps or storage, position approved sprinkler coverage, or issue a fire certificate. A portable powder extinguisher is intentionally not connected to the water sprinkler network.

Ventilation routing is geometric. It does not calculate room heat loads, duct pressure loss, noise, air changes, condensation, smoke-control performance or fire-damper requirements. Refrigerant routes do not establish a compatible indoor/outdoor pair, safe refrigerant charge, permitted elevation/length, insulation thickness or commissioning procedure. Catalogue claims about efficiency are not converted into simulated energy savings.

Plumbing routes retain real lengths and elevations but are not hydraulic design approval. Data/electrical coordination does not calculate cable fill, voltage drop, protection, fault level, PoE budget, segregation, bandwidth or cyber-security configuration. Port compatibility is a planning guard, not proof of operational connection. A camera model offers no live feed or surveillance coverage simulation.
