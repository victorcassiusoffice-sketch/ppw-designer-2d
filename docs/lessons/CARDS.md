# Lessons: the card for every tool and mode (English)

Generated from `src/i18n/lessonMessages.ts` by `tools/lessons/cards-doc-2026-11-08.mjs`. French, Simplified Chinese and Spanish are in the same file. On a touch screen a step shows its touch wording when it differs.

## Walls

Draw walls corner by corner. Close the shape to make a room.

- Click to drop a corner.  
  Touch: Tap to drop a corner.
- Or drag to draw a straight wall.
- Click the first corner to close the room.  
  Touch: Tap the first corner to close the room.

Keys: Enter closes · Esc stops · + and − change the step

## Room

Make a box room at an exact size.

- Pick Room, then type the length and width.
- Or use the 5 × 4 m room button.
- In 3D, drag from corner to corner.

## Doors and windows

Cut a door, doorway or window into a wall.

- Choose Door, Doorway or Window.
- Move along a wall, then click to place it.  
  Touch: Tap a wall to place it.
- Click a placed one to change or remove it.  
  Touch: Tap a placed one to change or remove it.

Keys: F flips the side · H swaps the hinge

## Stairs

Join two floors with a stair.

- Add a floor above first.
- Pick Stairs, then click the floor to place them.  
  Touch: Pick Stairs, then tap the floor to place them.
- The stair goes up to the next floor.

## Remove

Take away a wall, door, floor or object.

- Point at it: it turns red.  
  Touch: Tap the wall or object to remove.
- Click to remove it. Undo brings it back.  
  Touch: Undo brings it back.
- Esc or Done stops removing.

Keys: J · Ctrl+Z undoes

## Measure

Set a wall to an exact length.

- Click a wall.  
  Touch: Tap a wall.
- Type the length you want.
- Press Enter or Apply.

Keys: M

## Floor

Lay a floor, tile by tile or the whole room.

- Pick a material.
- Click a tile, or drag over an area.  
  Touch: Tap a tile, or drag over an area.
- Room covers the whole room.  
  Touch: Fill room covers the whole room.

Keys: Shift fills the room · Ctrl erases

## Wall paint

Paint one wall, or every wall of a room.

- Pick a paint and a colour.
- Click a wall to paint it.  
  Touch: Tap a wall to paint it.
- Room paints every wall of that room.

## Cladding

Cover a wall with sample boards.

- Pick a sample board.
- Click a wall to clad it.  
  Touch: Tap a wall to clad it.
- Room clads every wall of that room.

## Select

Pick up, move, turn or delete what you placed.

- Click an object to pick it.  
  Touch: Tap an object to pick it.
- Drag it to move it.
- The buttons beside it turn, copy or delete it.

Keys: H picks Select · Esc lets go

## Your object

Turn, copy, light or delete the object you picked.

- R turns it 45°.  
  Touch: Rotate turns it 45°.
- D makes a copy. L switches a lamp.  
  Touch: Duplicate makes a copy.
- Del removes it. Esc lets go.  
  Touch: Delete removes it.

Keys: R · D · L · Del

## Furnish

Add products: furniture, gym kit, lights and more.

- Pick a category, or search.
- Drag a product onto the floor.  
  Touch: Tap a product, then tap the floor.
- It sits flush to walls and corners.

## Copy

Copy an object you placed, then place more of it.

- Press E.
- Click an object to copy it.
- Click the floor to place the copy.

Keys: E

## Garden

Lay lawn, paths, a pool and fences outside.

- Pick a surface or a fence.
- Drag two corners to draw it.
- Nothing is added until you let go.

## 3D House

See your design in 3D, at real size.

- Drag to turn around the house.  
  Touch: Drag with one finger to turn around.
- Scroll to zoom. Shift-drag to move.  
  Touch: Pinch to zoom. Two fingers move the view.
- 2D Plan takes you back.

## Walk

Walk through your home as a person.

- Pick a person, then Start walk.
- WASD or the arrows move. The mouse looks.  
  Touch: The pad moves you. Drag to look.
- E opens doors. Esc leaves.  
  Touch: Tap a door button to open it.

Keys: WASD · E · Esc

## The first-build quest

- Build a room
- Add a door and a window
- Paint a wall
- Lay a floor
- Place three products
- Look at it in 3D
- Walk through it

Offer: New here? Build your first home in 7 short steps.

End: First build done! You built it, finished it, furnished it and walked through it.

## Loading tips

- Esc puts any tool away.
- With Floor, Shift fills the whole room.
- Ctrl+Z undoes your last step.
- Press R to turn the object you picked.
- Measure (M) sets a wall to an exact length.
- Wall paint: Room paints every wall at once.
- In Walk, E opens doors.
- The ? button lists every lesson.
- On a phone, press and hold a tool to see its name.
