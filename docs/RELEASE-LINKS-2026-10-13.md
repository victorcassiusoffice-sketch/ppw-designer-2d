## Claims to keep accurate

City Build coordinates measured layouts, infrastructure references, energy scenarios and dated supplier-equipment drafts. The same project retains the detailed house tools. Model envelopes are dimensional planning representations, not certified manufacturer CAD. Dates and recipients are draft planning fields: no automatic purchase order, invoice or email is sent. Private merchant draft persistence needs its database migration and deployed validation. Supplier references do not imply live inventory, a partnership or approved suitability. Road, structure, power, water and data-centre compliance need the relevant professionals.

For wider company pitches, catalogue onboarding, staff/client permissions, ERP/stock feeds, scheduled order release and company-specific integrations must be scoped and verified. Do not represent them as universally active by sharing the demonstration.
