# Engineering review and eco inputs — 15 October 2026

## Purpose and boundaries

The project review is a deterministic, versioned screening engine, not structural analysis or certification. It reports measured conflicts, omitted inputs and assumptions for an engineer to resolve. A complete screening run is still **unverified**. References supplied by a user are recorded as references, not authenticated evidence. No bearing capacity, reinforcement adequacy, wind resistance, pipe/cable sizing, permit approval, carbon offset or structural safety is inferred.

The canonical property retains its existing rooms, dimensions, quantity settings and products. New optional `eco` and `engineering` records are saved with pages, local persistence and history. Cloud snapshots validate their complete schemas without a database migration. Empty or old projects do not gain invented eco measurements.

## Primary sources inspected

1. **BS 5837:2012**, sections 4.3, 4.6, 5.3, 7.7 and Annex D, in the [public copy linked by North Somerset Council](https://n-somerset.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2020-02/BS%205837-2012.pdf). The single-stem circular screening radius is twelve times stem diameter, with the standard's nominal 15 m maximum. Actual root geometry can be asymmetric; soil and tree surveys inform changes. The software offers this explicitly named method and a user-entered surveyed radius. It does not extend the single-stem shortcut to veteran trees or invent a multi-stem measurement. This UK guidance is not asserted to be Mauritius law.
2. **Natural England/Forestry Commission**, [ancient and veteran tree planning advice](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/ancient-woodland-ancient-trees-and-veteran-trees-advice-for-making-planning-decisions). Veteran-tree buffers need special assessment, so the normal shortcut returns unknown when the veteran flag is set. The application does not prescribe a substitute buffer for Mauritius.
3. **Optigrün**, [technical planning guide, structural requirements](https://www.optigruen.com/fileadmin/02_DOKUMENTE/Broschueren/EN/Planungsunterlage_Optigruen_EN.pdf), section labelled page 126. Saturated green-roof assembly and retained water require explicit structural consideration; dry weight also matters for wind behaviour. No generic manufacturer's mass is copied into a project as a default. The full PDF exceeded the web reader's size limit; its indexed structural-requirements extract was inspected.
4. **Bauder**, [green roof design considerations](https://www.bauder.co.uk/technical-centre/standards-and-knowledge/green-roof-design-considerations). The manufacturer's guide identifies saturated components, waterproofing, vegetation, imposed and point loads, wind effects and slope-related restraint as design considerations. It directs structural suitability to the structural engineer. The app's scalar added-load comparison covers none of those combined design checks.
5. **Mauritius Ministry of Local Government**, [Building and Land Use Permit Guide, June 2013](https://localgovernment.govmu.org/MyDocument/Legislations/Building%20and%20Land%20Use%20Permit%20Guide.pdf), especially the residential documentation pages 36–37. It describes permit documentation, specified circumstances requiring registered professional engineering input and electricity, water and wastewater clearances. The [current ministry legislation/guidelines index](https://localgovernment.govmu.org/Pages/Legislations_Guidelines_Schemes.aspx) still links this guide alongside later amendments. The application therefore prompts current project-specific authority review; it does **not** turn dated numerical thresholds into an automated permit decision.

All sources checked on 15 October 2026. The source identity, URL and check date are also carried in review JSON. No copyrighted standard tables or drawings are copied into the application.

## Persisted contract and calculations

- `Property.eco`: version 1, at most 300 trees and 100 green-roof assemblies. IDs must be unique. One assembly per roof-room reference prevents accidental duplicate coverage records. Input bounds are editor limits, not environmental recommendations.
- Trees use ground-plan metres: trunk x/y, measured height and canopy radius, existing/proposed status, optional species text, stem diameter in millimetres, and explicit protection method. Missing survey/diameter data stays unknown. Procedural geometry is a measured envelope, not a verified species model.
- Root screening compares the circular envelope with the full foundation **excavation** footprint when available, otherwise the concrete footprint. It also checks below-ground household service routes and City utility trench width. Household pipe geometry does not contain trench dimensions, so those findings state that the full installation excavation remains unknown. Depth-dependent root distribution and soil shrinkage are not inferred.
- Canopy overlap is a plan coordination alert against building polygons below the entered tree height; it is not asserted to be an exact branch/roof collision.
- Green roof: the selected roof-room's polygon area, or a smaller explicit coverage area, multiplies manufacturer-entered saturated kg/m². Water entered in `extraRetainedWaterMm` must be **excluded** from that saturated figure: 1 mm over 1 m² adds approximately 1 kg. Added kPa = kg/m² × 9.80665 / 1000. Unknown mass/capacity stays null. Missing/deleted roofs and oversize coverage withhold total mass. Pitched roofs are flagged because the quantity uses horizontal plan area.
- `additionalLoadCapacityKPa` means an engineer's **additional permanent-load allowance**, not total roof capacity. Exceeding it is an explicit conflict. Staying below it never establishes safety. Assembly/reference, capacity/reference, waterproofing, drainage, wind, maintenance and point loads still require review.
- `Property.engineering`: version 1 with optional geotechnical report, structural design, concrete grade, reinforcement grade and utility design references. There is deliberately no `approved` field.
- `reviewProject(property)` returns JSON version 1 / engine 1.0.0, stable entity-linked findings, severity, evidence values/units, missing data, assumptions, recommendations and source IDs. It is deterministic and does not mutate the property. A cap of 100,000 geometry segment comparisons and 2,000 ordinary findings protects the browser; a visible critical `review-limit` finding and `screeningComplete:false` expose unfinished work.

## Demonstrated calculation fixes

1. **Solar annual aggregation:** previous code averaged twelve monthly daily outputs equally, treating February like a 31-day month. It now weights each monthly daily figure by month duration. The existing typical-year convention remains 365.25 days, using February 28.25. An incomplete non-empty monthly series is rejected. This is annual energy arithmetic, not an hourly PV yield simulation.
2. **Solar area precision:** previous code rounded a product footprint to two decimal places before computing capacity. A 1.004 m² panel could become 1 m², allowing two into a 2 m² allowance. Full physical area is now retained for capacity arithmetic; display formatting remains a UI concern. The existing 85% usable-roof factor remains an illustrative allowance, not a verified packing, setback or cyclone-mounting design.
3. **Foundation steel completeness:** a clear run longer than stock could previously produce a complete takeoff with zero lap allowance. Such element quantities are now withheld until a specified splice allowance or adequate stock length is entered. The procurement result remains incomplete even if all remaining concrete is priced. Existing roof logic already flagged this condition. No lap length or bar grade is invented.

## Regression coverage and continuation

Focused checks cover eco normalization and unknowns, roof mass/area/unit conversion, plan-distance conflicts (including excavation margins and corner distance), upper-floor service exclusions, veteran handling, stale roof links, deterministic machine-readable review, explicit work limits, atomic invalid edits, page/load/local-storage round trips, history/clear behaviour, server snapshot validation, solar arithmetic, foundation volumes and steel completeness, material prices/quantities, paint coats and service topology.

The existing paint algorithm multiplies net area by the selected whole coat count before final pack rounding; existing shared-wall/opening and material union tests passed. Service length uses the measured polyline and entered elevation difference; it does not size pressure pipes or electrical protection. No speculative rewrite was made where the audited arithmetic remained correct.

Next professional extensions require actual source data: asymmetric surveyed root polygons; soil and geotechnical models; engineer-approved bending schedules; demand/pressure and cable/protection models; hourly irradiance and obstruction data; system-specific green-roof assemblies and drainage design. These should extend the versioned inputs and review rules without relabelling screening as certification.
