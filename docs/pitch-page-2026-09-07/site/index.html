Try the maths yourself

The same formulas the designer runs — right here.

Nothing is estimated by eye. Every count below is the designer's own calculation, with the real Mauritian prices it uses today.

Paint, by the tin

Permoglaze Matt Emulsion (Sofap) — 9 m² per litre, two coats.

—

Wall area minus doors (0.84 × 2.04 m) and windows (1.2 × 1.2 m) × 2 coats ÷ 9 m²/L, then the cheapest whole-tin fill from 1 L Rs 201.25 · 5 L Rs 760 · 20 L Rs 2,940.

Floor, by the tile

Gym Interlock Tile 92 × 92 cm — Rs 1,600 per tile.

—

Whole tiles, plus the cut tiles along the walls, plus a 10 % offcut allowance on the cut tiles — the count the designer puts in the basket.

Solar, against the Mauritius sun

Jinko 475 W panel (Emcar) — Rs 12,075 each. Tamarin, 20° north-facing roof.

—

Generation = watts-peak × 5.17 sun-hours × 0.775 performance ratio (PVGIS SARAH3 2005–2023, Tamarin). Loads are the designer's sourced average draws.

What we handle

The questions a director asks first.

What does it cost us in effort?

One catalogue export and one review. You give us a product list, photos and dimensions; we produce the to-scale top-down image each product needs, the sizing and the pricing.

Couldn't our web team build this?

Building it in-house means a drawing engine, real-scale placement, tile and paint arithmetic, and a solar model tuned to Mauritius. The retailers who "built their own" — IKEA, Williams-Sonoma — bought a specialist company to do it. This one is finished and running today.

How long to launch?

Weeks, not months. The catalogue size sets the pace; we agree the go-live date at the walkthrough and hold to it.

Does it change our checkout?

No. Your checkout stays yours. The designer hands the basket, or a costed plan, to whichever you prefer.
