# Eco sheet estimates

- Rain (mm, January to December): [Mauritius Meteorological Services via Statistics Mauritius, mean rainfall by region](https://data.govmu.org/dataset/3690f341-b8dd-47b3-b73c-fa2055cc961c/resource/b27f5eaf-e15b-4cb5-a28b-515b2adeda80/download/mean-rainfall-by-region-and-by-month-2017-2021.pdf). Use the **1991–2020 long-term mean** column, not the individual year's observations. These five regions describe climates, not individual properties. Rounded monthly means may differ from official annual totals.
- A millimetre of rain over one square metre equals one litre. Use the measured projected roof outline including overhang; coating area uses the roof pitch. Room finish area deducts openings and omits deleted edges and glass. Bagasse block also omits concrete.
- Starting scenario, **to be measured in the pilot**: 2 people; runoff 0.8; non-potable demand 45 L/person/day; recoverable greywater 15 L/person/day; garden and planted wall 2 L/m²/day; permeable surface infiltration 0.5. These are editable study values, not local consumption observations. Generic tank sizes are capacity scenarios. Placed tanks use the existing catalogue capacity, not bounding-box volume.
- Distribute rain and demand over a 365-day year with uniform days within each month. Tank starts empty; limit inflow to tank capacity before serving demand. Greywater serves irrigation; stored rain serves household non-potable demand first. Infiltration does not go into the tank or reduce mains demand. A daily rain series may show longer dry spells.
- Recycled aggregate share starts at zero. The existing measured concrete estimate provides aggregate volume, including foundation and concrete work. The scenario bulk density of 1.5 t/m³ and chosen displacement share are **to be measured in the pilot**. No material performance or energy savings are claimed for changing a finish.
- Energy: placed-product ratings and user-entered operating hours feed the existing energy report. Studies are estimates only, not an approval.
