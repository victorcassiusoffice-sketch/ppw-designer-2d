## Scope and verification

The shared model stores real metre coordinates and per-node elevations; route quantities include vertical lengths. Visibility changes presentation only. Equipment references distinguish verified dimensions from schematic user-owned envelopes and unconfirmed local availability. The guide is a design-review sequence, with locally saved progress, not site instructions or approval. Circular bores are excavation-only; overlapping totals are withheld where an exact union is unsupported.

These tools do not provide hydraulic sizing, HVAC duty selection, engineered fire coverage, electrical protection design or regulatory certification. Powder extinguishers are standalone devices, not water-connected sprinklers. Cross-floor risers need separate measured coordination; there is no automatic cross-floor network solution.
